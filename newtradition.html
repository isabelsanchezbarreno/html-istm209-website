Veos in Trees

"Veos in Trees," the newest tradition on campus, is a creative twist to address students leaving unlocked electric bikes. When a bike is found unlocked, it gets a playful "parking" spot in a tree. This humorous reminder highlights the importance of e-bike security. As Aggies, we care about everyone's safety on campus, but we also care about the safety of electric bikes. "Veos in Trees" is a lighthearted way to promote safety on campus! Everyone is welcome to participate, but remember to prioritize safety when moving the bikes.

Place

All around the Texas A&M Campus

Time

Everyday, every hour

Invite

Everyone is welcomed to participate in this new tradition, just be safe!
