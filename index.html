Texas A&M University holds a moving ceremony called Silver Taps to honor students who passed away since the last ceremony. Held on the first Tuesday of each month, from September to April, the day starts with flags at half-mast and memorials placed for the fallen students. Throughout the day, students can write letters of condolence to their families. The most impactful part of the ceremony happens that night. At 10:15 pm, the campus plunges into darkness while hymns play from the bell tower. Students and families gather silently in the Academic Plaza. The Ross Volunteer company marches slowly, followed by a three-volley salute. Finally, buglers play a special rendition of "Taps" called "Silver Taps," three times in different directions a somber reminder that for these Aggies, the sun will never rise again. This unique tradition has been honoring fallen students since 1898.
